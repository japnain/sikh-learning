## Scope, omissions and interpretation

Selects the encounter, daily service and succession, omitting other episodes between chapters. Retains the reported affliction and the Guru's predictions of recovery and a green peg as traditional claims; the inspected passage does not separately narrate their fulfilment. Omits devotional actions not needed for this short reading.

Reading revision 2 changes p6's unsupported family relationship to the source's weavers. The account note now distinguishes predicted recovery and growth from reported fulfilment.
