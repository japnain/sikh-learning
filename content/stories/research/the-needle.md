## Scope, omissions and interpretation

The preceding account of Duni Chand's father's rebirth as a wolf is omitted and the following hymn condensed. The wife remains unnamed. Currency figures are those of the traditional account, not an audited financial record.

The existing narrative remains unchanged in this metadata revision. Descriptive closing sentences are original editorial interpretation, not new events or historical quotations.
