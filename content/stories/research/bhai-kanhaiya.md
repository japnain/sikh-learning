# Bhai Kanhaiya: acquisition record

Prepared: 23 October 2026. This is an acquisition memo, not a completed source review or a publishable biography.

## What is established

Punjabi University's [*Parchi Bhai Kanhaiya* entry](https://eos.learnpunjabi.org/PARCHI%20BHAI%20KANHAIYA.html) identifies a work by Bhai Sahaj Ram, composed between 1728 and 1740, with twenty sakhis. It identifies Bhai Hira Singh Mahant's edited publication by the Sevapanthi Addan Shahi Sabha, Patiala, in 1966. The entry describes the work's spiritual emphasis and relative scarcity of biographical detail.

The university's [Bhai Kanhaiya biography](https://eos.learnpunjabi.org/KANHAIYA%20BHAI%20%281648-1718%29.html) is another bibliographic starting point. Neither entry is the full source collection. The date of an early work does not establish rights to a later English translation.

## Retrieval attempted

Focused web searches used the phrases “Parchi Bhai Kanhaiya English translation text” and “Bhai Kanhaiya Sahaj Ram pdf”. Results supplied encyclopedia/catalog references, material about Bhai Addan Shah, and an encyclopedia volume on Internet Archive. They did not produce an inspectable English edition of the twenty-sakhi work during this task.

No full original-language edition or English translation of *Parchi Bhai Kanhaiya* has therefore been read here. This limited search does not establish that an English version does not exist. It establishes that the planned long profile currently lacks the promised inspected primary passages.

Use the expansion plan's **Bhai Mardana fallback** for the current pilot set.
