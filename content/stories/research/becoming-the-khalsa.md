## Significant editorial decisions and variants

1. **Separate early witness from later ceremony narrative.** The exact five-head sequence is not narrated in Sainapati's chapters V–VII. It appears here only as Macauliffe's later traditional account. The early poem's testimony should never be used to certify every later dramatic detail.
2. **No reconstruction inside the enclosure.** Macauliffe supplies a goat explanation. Other tellings treat the hidden action differently. The present piece recounts the volunteers and their appearance before the gathering while explicitly declining to reconstruct unseen action. An editor should decide whether the account sheet needs a fuller variant note before release.
3. **No crowd estimate.** Macauliffe gives a very large number and invokes a report attributed to a Persian historian/newswriter. This draft does not establish independent access to that purported report and does not repeat its count or present it as a separately authenticated contemporary document.
4. **No exact Gregorian day.** The draft uses Vaisakhi 1699. Differences between calendrical conversions, text headings and dates reported in later accounts were not independently resolved here.
5. **No identity claim about adding sweets.** Macauliffe names Mata Jito in his version; common retellings differ over association and framing. The piece omits that episode rather than silently substituting another name. It makes no claim about a complete history of women's contribution to the Khalsa.
6. **No fighting-sparrows miracle.** Macauliffe's p. 94 episode is omitted for focus. Its omission is editorial selection, not a declaration that every other line is independently verified.
7. **Careful language for other communities.** The source's pejorative, colonial, caste-hierarchical and broad religious judgments are omitted. Describing opposition recorded in a source is not an accusation against all Hindus or Muslims.
8. **No invented private state.** The prose contains no exact thoughts, newly coined historic speech, sensory decoration or named household participant absent from the source. Interpretive links remain narrator's explanation of the recorded actions.
9. **No list asserted as the complete modern initiation rite.** The selected conduct and articles are attributed to the narrative's account; modern Sikh guidance needs its own properly sourced treatment.
