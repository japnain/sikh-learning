## Scope, omissions and interpretation

Condenses the long opening theological exchange and watches-of-night hymn. The unnamed villager is not given an invented biography. Shaikh Brahm/Ibrahim, custodian of Farid's shrine, is distinguished from the earlier Shaikh Farid.
