Checked 23 October 2026 by the implementing AI, separately from the fresh English and history/Gurmat reviewers. This is a visual check of a named edition, not a human specialist review or an independent translation.

## Witness and method

Source: [Sainapati, Sri Gur Sobha, English translation by Kulwant Singh, Institute of Sikh Studies, Chandigarh, 2014](https://library.gurmat.info/download/sri-gur-sobha-sainapati-translation-kulwant-singh.pdf). The title, imprint and contents were visually inspected. The imprint identifies ISBN 81-85815-35-6.

The actual PDF was downloaded to `tmp/story-source-scans/sobha/`. PDFKit rendered the pages into PNGs at up to 1400 by 2000 pixels; each of the 32 pages listed below was opened with the image viewer and read. A PDF text-extraction response and web screenshot placeholders were not counted as image inspection. Rendering emitted a generic CoreGraphics warning, but the inspected pages display readable English, Gurmukhi, stanza numbers and printed page numbers.

This is a targeted comparison for `content/stories/drafts/becoming-the-khalsa.json`, reading revision 2. It is not a complete visual audit of the 432-page edition.

## Findings for the pilot

The selected passages support the pilot's brief comparison: attachment to the Guru rather than masands, disagreement over discipline, the death-related dispute, interrupted trade, appeals, reopened shops and reconciliation. The chapter introduction explains the omission of the familiar initiation drama. That editorial observation is distinct from the poem and does not disprove an event by silence. No story-prose correction is required by this visual comparison.

Two typesetting inconsistencies matter for future locators:

- On printed p. 64, the original/transliterated stanza ending in global 141 repeats local number 24; the facing English p. 65 labels it 25. Cite **V, global 141, pp. 64–65**, rather than relying on that duplicated local number alone.
- On English p. 113, local VII.4 and VII.5 have global numbers 264 and 265. The facing original/transliteration p. 112 gives 263 and 264; VII.6 is 265. Cite chapter-local numbering together with original global numbers and printed pages.

The existing dossier uses the correct original global ranges. These differences are recorded rather than silently copied into new citations. No translated lines are reproduced in this report or added to the pilot; the PDF remains a research witness, not a licence to republish the modern translation.
