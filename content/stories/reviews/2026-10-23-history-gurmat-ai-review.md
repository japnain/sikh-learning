**No unresolved mandatory historical or Gurmat correction remains in the versions covered by the final scoped follow-up below.** The initial text-based pass did not identify a mandatory correction; the subsequent scan review identified two small Amar Das source-precision changes and several locator refinements, which the implementing editor applied and this reviewer checked. The optional suggestions below improve clarity or completeness; they are not conditions for theological certification.

The three existing Gurbani anchors resolve to the intended compositions. They support the identified textual images and teachings. Their presence does not independently authenticate a childhood ceremony, a particular encounter with Sajjan, a detailed kitchen timetable or any miraculous event. The metadata already makes this distinction.

The stories' criticism concerns caste ranking, exploitation, hypocrisy or the particular narrated practice. I found no baseline sentence that assigns moral inferiority to all Hindus, Muslims, Brahmans, pilgrims or members of a hereditary occupation. The later source's broad polemical and colonial language has generally been avoided without replacing the plot with invented events.

This is a source and interpretation assessment within an AI pass. Human Sikh history/Gurmat review remains pending. A correct citation, a scan comparison and this report do not themselves establish every event as historically verified.

## Per-story findings

### 1. The Bread at Lalo's Table — public `bhai-lalo`

**Verdict: pass for the stated traditional-sakhi treatment; no required edit.** The contrast concerns how wealth is acquired and used. The narrator does not endorse caste ranking. The milk/blood claim is locally attributed to the tradition, and the conclusion does not add an undocumented repentance scene for Malik Bhago.

Unresolved evidence: this remains a selected later account without an independently examined Janamsakhi recension comparison or event corroboration. Additional websites repeating Macauliffe would not resolve that limit. No invented variant should be added to fill the gap.

### 2. The Water He Carried — public `guru-amar-das-service`

**Verdict: pass with the existing explicit traditional attribution; no required factual or theological edit.** Service, Bibi Amro's role and succession are connected without an added private conversation. P6 already identifies the woman's affliction and the green peg as miraculous features of the account. It must not be reported as an unqualified medical or historical statement.

Optional H1: if the English editor softens the dated phrase “lost her reason”, replace p6's second sentence with:

> It says that she became mentally unwell after the rebuke.

Retain the opening attribution and the following recovery/green-peg claims. This preserves the account without introducing a diagnosis or presenting illness as a general consequence of disrespect. Keeping the current clearly attributed wording is also defensible. Silently removing the miracle or replacing it with invented psychological symptoms would change the selected telling.

Unresolved evidence: the supernatural causation and recovery are not independently established. This review does not derive a general rule about illness from that narrative.

### 3. The Shade of a Shared Table — public `mata-khivi`

**Verdict: pass as a short profile with an early textual anchor and later biographical context; no required edit.** The account correctly separates the community's routine from an undocumented personal schedule for Khivi. Its modest length reflects the brief direct testimony. It does not imply that Khivi personally taught Amro the particular hymn heard by Amar Das.

Optional H2: replace the first sentence of the account note with the following to make the direct primary anchor more visible:

> This short profile draws on the praise of Mata Khivi in the Guru Granth Sahib, Ang 967, and later biographical context in Macauliffe's 1909 account.

The existing source list already supplies this distinction, so the change is clarification. Unresolved evidence: exact supervisory arrangements, workers, personal instructions and a detailed daily timetable remain undocumented by the inspected material.

### 4. The House That Hid Its Darkness — public `sajjan`

**Verdict: pass for the stated traditional-sakhi treatment; no required edit.** The Ang 729 reference identifies the correct six-stanza shabad. Its use of friendship and outward appearance is relevant to this reading, while the historical setting remains attributed to the later narrative. The conclusion treats confession and material action together without claiming that giving away property restores victims' lives or settles a modern legal question.

Unresolved evidence: the meeting, Sajjan's individual criminal history and the establishment of a dharmsal are narrative claims, not independently authenticated events or archaeology. The account note already preserves this limit.

### 5. The Milk and the Gold — public `the-milk-and-the-coin`

**Verdict: pass for the stated traditional-sakhi treatment; no required edit.** The account distinguishes Shaikh Brahm/Ibrahim from the earlier Farid. The villager is presented as generous before his fear appears; the narrative does not make material wealth proof of spiritual attainment. The prose account of wakefulness and divine gift does not turn devotion into a guaranteed transaction.

Optional future enrichment: Ang 1384, saloks 112–113, could be added as a limited textual anchor after a dedicated authorship/heading check. This review verified the corresponding text but does not use those lines to prove the milk encounter or assign the speakers independently of the sakhi. No new source record is required merely to increase the citation count.

Unresolved evidence: both gold wonders and the particular dialogue occasion remain traditional claims.

### 6. A Needle for the Next World — public `the-needle`

**Verdict: pass for the stated traditional-sakhi treatment; no required edit.** The wife retains a substantive role without an invented name or biography. The ending concerns generosity and remembrance, not a claim that financial giving mechanically purchases liberation. The omission of the preceding rebirth episode is disclosed rather than silently rejected as inauthentic.

Unresolved evidence: the wealth display and seven-lakh figure are reported details of the selected telling, not corroborated financial records. This review does not establish an exact year or audited fortune.

### 7. A Thread That Would Not Break — public `the-sacred-thread`

Unresolved evidence: the primary composition does not by itself date a childhood ceremony, identify its priest or verify a reconstructed exchange. The account/editorial notes already distinguish these questions. No precise childhood age has been newly asserted in the reading.

### 8. Water Turned West — public `water-at-haridwar`

### 9. Becoming the Khalsa — draft `becoming-the-khalsa`

**Verdict: pass within the limited source-aware historical-profile treatment, subject to the separate English edits; no mandatory historical correction.** The later ceremony telling and the earlier poem remain distinct. The discipline and community's relation to the Guru are not reduced to either military display or the removal of intermediaries alone. Reciprocal initiation is central, and the text does not assert that caste prejudice instantly vanished from society.

The particular volunteer ordering and normalized names agree with Macauliffe's selected version. Other familiar orderings do not by themselves make this one a transcription error. The quoted source speeches are not promoted to eyewitness transcripts. The brief account of trade closure and reconciliation is supported by the cited *Sri Gur Sobha* passages.

The English review's proposed replacement of five-3 is acceptable historically: it preserves the volunteers' reappearance while leaving the enclosure selection rationale in the note. Removing a method aside from the body must not delete the existing account/editorial distinction between a later traditional telling and an independently verified ceremony.

Optional H3: improve the completeness of bowl-1 without deciding the disputed contributor's identity. Replace its first three sentences with:

> In Macauliffe's account, the initiation water was stirred in an iron vessel with a khanda, a double-edged sword, while sacred compositions were recited. Sweets were added before the five received the amrit.

The source explicitly includes sweets on printed p. 95; uncertainty over the person associated with adding them does not require omission of the ingredient. The current selected summary does not claim to prescribe the complete rite, so the omission is not treated as a factual error. Retain the rest of the paragraph and update the note if this suggestion is accepted so it no longer implies that the entire sweets episode is absent.

Unresolved evidence: the unseen enclosure action, exact attendance and Gregorian date have not been independently authenticated. Sainapati's different narrative emphasis neither certifies every later detail nor disproves an event by silence. The chosen comparison is brief; it is not a recension history or a complete account of present-day rahit.

### 10. The Music Beside the Journey — draft `bhai-mardana`

**Verdict: pass as an explicitly qualified profile assembled from traditional episodes; no mandatory historical correction.** Musical skill, questions and practical companionship give Mardana agency. The final-rites variant is disclosed in the body. There is no unsupported conversion claim or assignment of the Ang 553 saloks' authorship.

Optional H4, consistent with the English review's M1: replace the first two sentences of a-song-heard with:

> In another traditional episode, the two men rested outside Pak Pattan. Shaikh Kamal, a disciple of Shaikh Brahm, was gathering firewood nearby.

This is a clarity choice. Macauliffe's chapter IX explicitly describes another visit; the current “On a later visit” is source-supported and must not be recorded as a fabricated chronology error. The neutral transition better foregrounds the selection of separate episodes.

Optional H5: make the relevant religious background visible without asserting a conversion outcome. Add this sentence after the first sentence of the-rabab:

> Mardana came from a Muslim family in Talwandi.

Punjabi University's entry explicitly supports this background, and Macauliffe also identifies a Muslim background. If accepted, add the university entry as an honestly scoped contextual source and revise the dossier's suggestion that identifying his background requires a binary conversion narrative. This is a useful addition to a life profile, not evidence that the existing text makes a false identity claim.

Unresolved evidence: final-rites traditions differ; Muslim family background does not settle Mardana's later identity in every source. Avoid inferring a conversion solely from the word “disciple”, or treating one later rite account as proof of a single religious classification. The account's scene order is not an independently established itinerary.

### 11. The Shade of a Shared Table — draft `mata-khivi`

**Verdict: pass within the stated evidence limits, subject to the separate English edit; no mandatory historical correction.** Ang 967 provides a direct early anchor, and Santbir Singh is credited for the particular interpretation of naming the Guru in relation to Khivi. Calling her contribution “leadership” is a reasonable attributed/editorial interpretation of substantial generosity and responsibility; it is not evidence of an independently documented administrative title or workforce.

The proposed English deletion of p4 removes repetition without losing a unique factual claim. The separate Amro passage remains valuable and does not invent maternal transmission of the particular hymn. The later household and community passages are not silently turned into Khivi's own speech or personal timetable.

Unresolved evidence: the source does not support a detailed reconstructed kitchen day, private orders, a complete family biography or a mother-to-daughter teaching scene. The current notes disclose these limits. This draft should not acquire an expert-approved label merely because this AI pass found its restraint appropriate.
