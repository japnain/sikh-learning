# Volume II source-image comparison — 23 October 2026

## Scope and method

This is an implementation-agent comparison with original-edition page images, not an independent English edit, Sikh history/Gurmat review, or certification of the narrated events. The comparison covers the public readings `mata-khivi` (revision 1) and `guru-amar-das-service` (revision 1), the expanded Khivi pilot (revision 3, 653 words, as read during this check), and their research dossiers.

All sixteen images listed below were opened with `view_image` and read visually. This report does not substitute OCR, Wikisource transcription, or page metadata for visual inspection. The images were acquired centrally from Internet Archive item [`thesikhreligioni02macauoft`](https://archive.org/details/thesikhreligioni02macauoft), identified in the project as Macauliffe's original 1909 *The Sikh Religion*, Volume II. Local acquisition paths and SHA-256 values are recorded in `tmp/story-source-scans/acquired/manifest.json`; the files inspected are in `tmp/story-source-scans/acquired/v2/`. No PDF was used.

The image endpoint uses zero-based `n` indices. In this particular scan, the inspected printed page is `n − 9`; the dossier's one-based Wikisource leaf is `n + 1`. Every displayed numbered header in the inspected set agrees with the printed-page locator. The chapter-opening page identified as printed p. 1 has no visible printed numeral; its text and following numbered page 3 establish its place in this set. This mapping is specific to this scan, not a general rule for other digitizations.

| Public p3; pilot p8–p9: offerings, travellers/pilgrims, line of diners, children and evening meal | Printed p. 15 explicitly places offerings in Guru Angad's kitchen for pilgrims and wayfarers. | Pilot p11: the Guru named in relation to Mother Khivi | This turn is visible in Macauliffe's translation on printed p. 27. The pilot attributes the modern contextual observation to Santbir Singh. | Public p6; pilot p12–p13: Amro sings, answers Amar Das, takes him to Guru Angad | Printed pp. 31–32 explicitly support the pre-dawn singing, her father's instruction, repeated hymn, request and later accompanied journey. No factual correction to either Khivi narrative was required by the inspected Macauliffe pages. The current pilot's use of leadership in its description remains an interpretation of named generosity and distribution, not documentation of an administrative title or formal kitchen office.

## Exact precision findings to apply

1. Public Amar Das p6 says, “When her family brought her to Guru Angad”. Printed p. 43 identifies the group as the weavers; it does not specify her family. Prefer “When the weavers brought her to Guru Angad”. This removes an unnecessary relationship inference.
2. The Amar Das narrative correctly says Guru Angad declared that the woman **would** recover and the peg **would** grow green. The account note and dossier describe recovery and the peg becoming green as miraculous details/events without consistently retaining this distinction. The inspected pp. 43–44 contain predictions, not a separate report of their fulfilment. Prefer “the reported affliction and the predictions of her recovery and of the peg growing green” in methodological notes. Do not claim these images independently verify a miracle or its occurrence.
3. Existing dossier statements that a complete scan-image comparison was not claimed can now be supplemented with this bounded sixteen-page comparison. They should not be replaced with an unqualified claim that all of Volume II, every cited external source, or the historical truth of every event has been verified.

These are the only discrepancies found in the assigned claim set. No JSON or dossier files were edited by this review agent; the parent editor owns their application.

## Limits retained

This review verifies fidelity to the inspected Macauliffe images, not historical corroboration beyond that work. It does not establish the date or authorship of every tradition, compare recensions, authenticate miraculous details, or complete a specialist evaluation of the interpretation of Gurbani. It does not independently recheck Ang 967's Gurmukhi images, the SriGranth digital transcription, or the modern SikhRI article. Human English and history/Gurmat review statuses remain pending. It does not authorize reproduction of entire scans or modern translations.
