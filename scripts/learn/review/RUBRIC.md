- `summary`: 1-2 sentences. Keep each sentence at 24 words or fewer. Name the emotional state the line meets rather than repeating the topic label. The best summaries feel like a true diagnosis and a true turn.
- `takeaway`: one sentence, 14 words or fewer. Make it concrete and memorable. End on a clear verb or precise noun, not a vague abstraction.
- `lifeApplication`: 1-2 sentences. Name a specific time, place, or body cue such as the mouth, the doorway, before the reply, before the message is sent, at the sink, or at the threshold. The move must be survivable in ordinary life, not idealized.

- `topic issueStatement`: name the real wound or pressure without melodrama. It should sound like a person under strain, not a textbook category.
- `topic centralInsight`: state the Guru's correction cleanly. One strong turn is better than three blurred ideas.
- `scenario title`: name the lived situation directly. It must feel usable in search and readable in the app.
- `scenario issueStatement`: name what the mind/body is doing in that scenario. It should sound immediate and embodied, not generic.
- `scenario centralInsight`: show how Gurbani re-frames that exact situation. It must stay smaller and more precise than the overview insight.
- `scenario practicalReflection` / `actionPrompt`: give one concrete move the reader can take next. It should feel humble, exact, and possible today.
- `scenario excerpt`: quote or paraphrase the cited line directly enough that the source is still visible.
- `scenario explanation`: make the excerpt matter in that specific situation. Do not repeat the translation or drift into decorative commentary.
