# Independent review of Bhagat selections and Vaars — 1 November 2026

A separate AI review examined all **46 assigned entries** in `baniDiscoveryBhagats.ts` (22) and `baniDiscoveryVaars.ts` (24). This is a contextual metadata review by reviewers separate from the original writers, not independent human Gurmat/English approval, a critical edition or certification of every scripture translation.

**Result:** all 22 Bhagat descriptions were supported by the inspected evidence. One Vaar entry needed a selection-boundary clarification: ID87 Majh Vaar. The correction has been implemented by the parent agent and inspected below. The other 23 Vaar entries were supported.

## What was checked

Every entry's English name, attribution, stated Angs, opening theme, anthology description and form/variant distinctions were compared with the real BaniDB snapshots captured on 31 October 2026. These include all captured source headings and page lists, the first ten lines and final five lines for each selected reading. Primary scripture pages were freshly consulted for material boundaries and specific interpretive/count claims. Existing research notes were orientation, not the sole evidence.

The review does not claim that every verse in all long selections was freshly retrieved or independently translated. Concise thematic summaries remain editorial interpretations grounded in the cited passages. Current BaniDB boundaries may change; retained snapshot hashes identify the selection reviewed.

## Correction implemented and checked

ID87's actual selection includes the first Gauri Guareri hymn of Guru Nanak after the Majh Vaar ends on Ang150. The captured last headings switch to Gauri/Mahalla1, and the tail reaches the next hymn's closing marker on Ang151. Primary Ang150/151 independently confirm that boundary. The final context now says:

> Its heading specifies the tune associated with Malik Murid and Chandrahara Sohiya. This reading also includes Guru Nanak Dev Ji’s following Gauri Guareri hymn on Ang 151, after the Majh Vaar ends.

The final entry also references Ang151 and the exact BaniDB87 reading. This correction accurately describes the supplied selection and leaves scripture and reading identity intact. No reason for the provider's inclusion has been invented.

**ID107 was specifically rechecked and needs no change.** The Fifth Guru chhant earlier on Ang1312 is outside the captured Kanara Vaar selection. BaniDB107 begins at the Fourth Guru Vaar heading and its Fourth Guru opening salok. A note claiming an included Fifth Guru prelude would be incorrect.
