# SGGS Vaars and Seven Days: research handoff

Implemented in `src/content/baniDiscoveryVaars.ts`; IDs, routes and selection boundaries are unchanged.

- IDs 50 and 51 use the weekday poetic form. They should not be described as two more of the twenty-two ballad-form Vaars.
- A Vaar’s heading identifies its principal author; accompanying saloks can belong to other Gurus. The copy preserves this distinction.
- ID 90 includes accompanying Fourth Guru chhants from Angs 448–451, interleaved with the Vaar. Do not trim it to Angs 462–475.
- ID 51 ends with the first ten-stanza sequence on Ang 842; the next Seven Days sequence is not part of this provider selection.
- ID 107 is the Fourth Guru’s Kanara Vaar. The Fifth Guru heading earlier on Ang 1312 belongs to a preceding chhant.

The JSON records each claim and its primary source. Context sentences describe verified opening material, avoiding invented summaries of an entire long composition.
