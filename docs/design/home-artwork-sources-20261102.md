Wikimedia Commons description pages identify these reproductions as public domain. The catalog preserves source links, artist/date information where known, and English/Punjabi/Hindi descriptions. Each complete composition was visually inspected; no subjects, borders or source inscriptions were cropped.

Every image has a separate 320px thumbnail. Only the selected full painting loads on Home; full additions are not part of the eager startup graph. The additions are scoped to Home and do not silently enter the share composer.

## Quality follow-up

The original source files above replace the initially shipped smaller derivatives. Home also replaces `devotional-interior` with the 1951 × 1400 museum-sourced WebP documented on [Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Watercolour_painting_of_the_interior_of_the_Golden_Temple_in_Amritsar,_by_William_Carpenter,_circa_1854.webp). Its history traces the improved scan to V&A and the old 832 × 592 derivative to The Heritage Lab. The painting was visually matched before replacement. A matching 320px thumbnail is generated for Home; the legacy share files are unchanged.

All Home frames now enforce a maximum width of one third of the actual source width, in addition to the individual composition cap. This guarantees at least 3 source pixels per CSS pixel without enlarging files or synthesizing detail. It does not establish that an unknown upstream source was never enlarged.

No verified superior full-composition source was found for String and tabla, Floral teacher mural, or Rider and attendants. The former two now display at maximum widths of 275.7px and 240px. Commons photographs of the Bairagi mural were positively identified but omit the existing outer floral border; a superficially similar Lal Singh mural was rejected as a different work. Alternate Rider captures and Fort procession copies offered no verified improvement. These originals remain source-limited and are not described as newly restored or higher-resolution files.
