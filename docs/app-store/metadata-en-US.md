## Description

```text
NaamRas is a calm, local-first Gurbani reading companion designed to keep scripture at the center.

Begin with a daily reading, browse complete banis, explore source-backed study routes, and return to saved passages without creating an account.

READ WITH CLARITY
• Browse Nitnem and a broad Gurbani catalog
• Keep Gurmukhi primary, with optional Devanagari, transliteration, and meanings
• Adjust text size, line spacing, alignment, contrast, and motion preferences
• Open word details through an accessible verse action

KEEP YOUR PLACE
• Save passages and bookmarks
• Build a personal vocabulary list
• Keep reading progress and preferences on your device

EXPLORE MORE
• Read curated library material, including Panth Prakash
• Browse Amrit Keertan and Rehat resources
• Use ambient study soundscapes when you want a quieter reading environment

NaamRas works as a guest. Version 1.0 has no subscriptions, in-app purchases, advertising, or required account.

Scripture and translation lookups use BaniDB-backed sources. Some reading content requires a network connection the first time it is opened.
```
